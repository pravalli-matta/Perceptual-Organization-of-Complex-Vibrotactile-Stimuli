# Perceptual Organization of Complex Vibrotactile Stimuli

## 📋 Project Overview

This project explores the perceptual organization of complex vibrotactile stimuli through systematic investigation of how humans perceive and categorize different vibrotactile patterns.

## 🎯 Research Focus

- **Perceptual Organization**: Understanding how complex vibrotactile stimuli are organized in human perception
- **Stimulus Characteristics**: Analysis of various vibrotactile parameters and their perceptual effects
- **Categorization**: How participants group and differentiate between different tactile patterns

## 📊 Methodology

The study employed:
- Systematic presentation of complex vibrotactile stimuli
- Participant rating and categorization tasks
- Statistical analysis of perceptual similarities
- Multidimensional scaling (MDS) to map perceptual spaces
